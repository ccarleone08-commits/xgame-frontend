Backgammon Rules

Move all 15 checkers into your home board and bear them off before your opponent.

01

Move with the dice

Use each die as a separate move. Doubles give four moves.

02

Open and blocked points

You cannot land on a point occupied by two or more opposing checkers.

03

Hit and re-enter

A single opposing checker can be hit. Checkers on the bar must re-enter first.

04

Bear off to win

Once every checker is home, bear them off. The first player to clear all 15 wins.
