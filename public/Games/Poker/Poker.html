Texas Hold'em Guide

Poker Rules

Build the strongest five-card hand using your two private cards and the five community cards.

Goal

Win the pot by having the best hand at showdown or by making every opponent fold.

Hand Flow

  • Each player receives two private hole cards.
  • Betting rounds: pre-flop, flop, turn, and river.
  • Five community cards are opened in the center.

Actions

  • Check when no bet is required.
  • Call to match the current bet.
  • Raise to increase the bet, or Fold to leave the hand.
