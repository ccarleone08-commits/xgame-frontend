Seka Guide

Seka Rules

Collect three cards, build the highest Seka score, and win the pot through betting or showdown.

Goal

Win the pot by staying in the hand with the strongest three-card score or by making your opponent fold.

Hand Flow

  • Each player receives three private cards.
  • Players take turns calling, raising, folding, or going all-in.
  • If players continue to the end, the cards open in showdown.

Scoring

  • Three aces are the strongest Seka hand.
  • Strong same-rank sets and suited point hands beat weaker scores.
  • The visible score badge shows your current hand value when cards are dealt.

Actions

  • Call matches the current bet and keeps you in the hand.
  • Raise increases pressure within the table limits.
  • Fold leaves the hand; Showdown compares cards when available.
