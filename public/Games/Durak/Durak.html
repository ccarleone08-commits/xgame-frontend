How To Play

Durak is won by the player who gets rid of all cards first.

Goal

Empty your hand before the other players and avoid being the last player with cards.

Round Flow

  • The attacker starts by placing a card on the table.
  • The defender must beat each attack with a higher same-suit card or any trump.
  • If the defender cannot or does not want to defend, the defender takes all table cards.
  • Additional attacks may only use ranks already visible on the table.
  • After the round, players draw back from the deck and the turn order continues.
Tip: Save your trump cards for key defenses and late rounds.
